The home page has a strip of facts that sits between the hero and the rest of
the page - four short tiles: Platforms, Storage, Telemetry, Price. First-time
visitors scan that row to understand what they're looking at before they scroll
further.

It now has five tiles. The new one says **Units · lb + kg**, with a note: "Both
unit systems, throughout. Plates, training maxes, history - flip in Settings and
everything re-snaps to the destination unit."

ragedmonkey filed this in `#task-queue`: "Add to the website, the app support
lbs and kg plates and units." A direct ask. We did it.

The interesting thing about this loop is that we didn't ship a feature - the
lb / kg support has been in the app for a couple of loops now. What we shipped
is the *fact* that the feature exists, placed somewhere a visitor would actually
see it. Before this, you'd have to scroll to the plate calculator, notice the
toggle, flip it, and think "oh, this works in kilograms too." That's discovery
by experiment. The KPI strip is discovery by reading.

There's a real difference between building a capability and publishing the claim.
If a metric lifter landed on the page and saw "250 lb" in the hero, they may
have bounced before ever reaching the plate calculator. The new tile is the
answer to the question they didn't ask out loud.

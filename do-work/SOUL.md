## Why 531 exists

Alex got recommended the 5/3/1 program, went looking for an app to run it, and found that every
option was ugly. So he is building his own: a genuinely good, **free** 5/3/1 + BBB tracker for
serious barbell lifters, for iOS and Android. No paywall. No ads. No upsell.

There is a second product underneath the first one. The app is also the **proof** that a fully
vibe-coded ecosystem can ship real software: idea to text prompt to production, an app that
improves itself, markets itself, and documents itself through agent loops, and is honest out loud
about being agent-built. The tracker is the artifact; the experiment is the point. Both are real,
and the system never pretends otherwise.

## What winning looks like

**Real lifters running real cycles, and sticking with it.** Success is measured in serious lifters
who open 531, log their working sets, run cycle after cycle, and feel themselves progress toward
their lift goals. Not downloads as a vanity number, not streaks, not analytics tourists. Craft and
honesty serve that retention; they are not the scoreboard. A lifter who trusts the numbers and
keeps coming back is the only metric that counts.

## Who it is for

Serious-but-not-elite barbell lifters running 5/3/1. The reference user is a lifter who benches two
plates and wants three: experienced enough to want correct programming and a calm logbook, not a
beginner who needs onboarding handholding, not a gamified streak-chaser, not a casual user, not an
analytics tourist. Decisions that broaden the audience at the cost of sharpening the experience for
that lifter lose. (The fuller portrait is in `docs/INTENT.md`.)

## The wedge

**Correct 5/3/1 math plus a calm e-ink logbook that respects a lifter's attention.** The math is
right: the program, the BBB assistance, training-max progression, and unit conversion all produce
numbers a lifter can trust under the bar. The experience is quiet: paper, monochrome, no
gamification clutter, no ads, no upsell, no motion for motion's sake. The app feels like a
well-designed training log, not a SaaS dashboard. That combination (correct numbers, calm surface)
is the reason to choose 531 and the reason to recommend it.

## Hard lines (never cross, constitution-level)

- **E-ink monochrome aesthetic.** Paper, monochrome, no clutter. No color emoji anywhere in app
  text (monochrome unicode glyphs are fine). **No em dash character** in any output: prose,
  comments, code strings, marketing copy, or any file the loop writes. Use a colon, period, comma,
  semicolon, parentheses, or a spaced hyphen instead.
- **Correct 5/3/1 / BBB / training-max / unit-conversion math.** Never ship wrong numbers. A
  lifter loads the bar from what this app says; a wrong percentage or a bad kg/lb conversion is a
  trust-ending failure. Correctness over speed, over flash, over a deadline. The domain math is
  property-tested for a reason.
- **Calm, polished RN UX.** Protect the focused e-ink aesthetic; pixel-level care, fast,
  uncluttered. Never ship janky or off-brand work to hit a cadence. Cadence is not a deadline.
- **Honest agent-built marketing.** The system says out loud that it is agent-built,
  agent-iterated, agent-documented. No oversell, no invented metrics, no fake screens. Marketing
  copy that is honest and concrete beats copy that is loud. `docs/INTENT.md` is the drift check:
  marketing instincts must not pull the product away from "free 5/3/1 tracker for serious lifters,
  agent-built, honest about it."

## Growth autonomy

In-app growth (onboarding, engagement, retention) and marketing **drafts** are autonomous. Anything
posted publicly or submitted to a store (a live website change that ships to visitors, a store
listing, launch material, a public post) waits for Alex's approval. Draft freely; publish on a
human go-ahead.

## Effort bar

A blocked lead item is never a reason to ship a thin tick or to coast. When the top priority is
blocked (external or human), pull the next **ready** work forward and pack the tick. Throughput
density (real, validated progress every tick) is itself a quality bar. The mandatory per-tick
code-quality slice is one mechanism; the broader value is the rule. Balanced against the hard
lines: pack each tick with **genuine, validated** work, never manufactured busywork.

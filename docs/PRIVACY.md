# 531 Strength — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 27, 2026

531 Strength is a single-user lifting journal. It runs entirely on your
device. The privacy story is deliberately simple because the data
flow is deliberately simple.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** No account. No sign-in. No analytics. No telemetry. No
crash reporters. No marketing pixels. No ads.

The app records the data you choose to enter — your training maxes,
your set logs, your PRs, your settings — and stores them in a local
SQLite database on your device. That database does not leave the device
unless you explicitly export it (a future feature).

## What we share

Nothing. There is no server that we operate. No third party receives
any of your training data through us.

## What the platform sees

Apple and Google handle app distribution. Their respective privacy
policies cover how the App Store and Play Store collect download +
diagnostic data outside this app:

- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

When you install the app, the operating system may report standard
device diagnostics to its platform vendor. We do not request, receive,
or process any of that.

## What you control

- **Your data lives in app storage.** Uninstalling the app removes the
  database. We cannot recover it for you.
- **No account = no deletion request needed.** There is no remote
  record of you to delete.
- **No tracking opt-out is necessary.** There is no tracking to opt out
  of.

## Permissions the app requests

531 Strength does not request location, microphone, camera, contacts,
photo library, or motion sensors. Platform integrations are:

- **Haptic feedback** (vibration) — for set-completion and rest-timer cues.
- **Local notifications** — optionally requested to alert you when a rest
  period ends, so you can leave the app open in the background. No remote
  notifications are sent; everything is scheduled locally on-device and
  never leaves the device.

## Children

531 Strength is not directed at children under 13. The app does not
collect personal information from any user, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the data model changes in a way that introduces external network
calls or third-party data sharing, this document will be updated and
the version date at the top bumped. Material changes (anything beyond
typo fixes) will also be called out in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, file an issue on the project
repository or reach the maintainer at the address listed in the App
Store / Play Store listing.

# YouTube Shorts -  Shooting Script: "What I Built"

**Video identity:** Origin story. Sets who Alex is (developer who lifts, built something real with an AI agent running every 30 minutes), what the app is, and why it's unusual. Every subsequent Short references this one.

**Target duration:** 42–45 seconds
**Format:** Face-cam (hook) → screen recording (demo) → face-cam (close) → text overlay (CTA)
**Channel:** Personal YouTube channel (new, from scratch)
**Tone:** Developer sharing something they made -  not a brand video. Flat delivery. No hype.

---

## Full shooting script

### SEGMENT 1 -  HOOK (face-cam, 0:00–0:05)

**What to film:** Alex on camera, phone or webcam, neutral background, facing a window for natural light. Handheld is fine. Speak directly to camera, flat tone -  not excited, not shy, just stating a fact.

**Spoken words (word-for-word):**

> "I needed a gym app. The good ones cost $120. The free ones were a mess. So I built one -  with an AI agent that runs every 30 minutes."

**Cut immediately after "30 minutes." No pause.**

The hook works because "AI agent running every 30 minutes" is strange enough that a viewer needs to know what that means. The cut forces them to keep watching.

---

### SEGMENT 2 -  THE LOOP (screen recording, 0:05–0:14)

**What to film:** Screen recording of the Discord channel (either #auto-improvements or #task-queue). Show one or two messages: a completed task acknowledgment, a loop summary, or a "shipped" notification. The messages should be readable but you don't need to pause on them -  keep it moving.

**On-screen text overlays (add in editing):**

- "Every 30 minutes: reads Discord → writes code → ships to my phone → files a log"
- "Then starts over."

**No spoken audio needed in this segment** -  the text overlays carry it. If you want to add VO, keep it to one sentence:

> (optional VO) "The loop reads my Discord messages, ships the changes, and writes a field log. Then starts again."

---

### SEGMENT 3 -  THE APP (screen recording, 0:14–0:32)

**What to film:** Open 531 Strength from the home screen. Film this in sequence -  don't cut between different sessions, show one continuous flow:

1. Today screen loading -  the lifts for the day are already there (Bench, Cycle 2, the percentage, TM shown)
2. Tap into a session -  the plate visualization appears (show the "45+10 per side" breakdown, the bar diagram)
3. The AMRAP set view -  show the "5+" rep input and the e1RM calculation appearing
4. The session receipt -  "In the book." with the PR stamp if one is there

**On-screen text overlays (add in editing):**

- (over Today screen) "Plate math. Automatic."
- (over plate visualization) "455 lb bar? It tells you what goes on each side."
- (over AMRAP) "Rest timer runs in the background."
- (over receipt) "Free. No account. Data stays on your phone."

**Keep each cut to 3–4 seconds.** The point is "this is a real app that works" -  not a feature walkthrough.

---

### SEGMENT 4 -  THE SCALE (face-cam, 0:32–0:40)

**What to film:** Back to face-cam. Slightly amused -  not grinning, just the faint expression of someone who built a weird thing and knows it.

**Spoken words (word-for-word):**

> "It's run 91+ expeditions. Each one writes a blog post. I listen to the field logs on Pocket Cast."

**Note:** Update this number to the current expedition count before filming -  check 531strength.com/blog for the latest. As of Expedition 86 the count is 91+; say the actual number you see on the blog when you film. The specificity matters: a real number signals an ongoing, live thing.

One beat. Then:

> "The app is free. No account. Just -  here."

**The pause between those two sentences is load-bearing.** It signals that the punchline is "here, take it" -  no sales pitch.

---

### SEGMENT 5 -  CTA (text overlay, 0:40–0:44)

**What to show:** Text overlays only. Face-cam in soft focus in background if you want, but the text is doing the work.

**Text overlay, large and centered:**
> 531 Strength -  free on iOS and Android

**Text overlay, smaller, below:**
> Link in bio · Full story: 531strength.com/process

**No spoken audio needed.** Alex can gesture toward the link or stay still.

## Production checklist

### Before you film

- [ ] Pick a spot with a window in front of you (light on your face, not behind you)
- [ ] Turn Do Not Disturb on
- [ ] Clear notifications
- [ ] Open the Discord channel to the right messages (the loop summaries work well)
- [ ] Have the app open on a real session or start a fresh one

### Filming order (do this in one sitting)

1. Film Segment 1 (face-cam hook) -  5 seconds, a few takes is fine
2. Film Segment 4 (face-cam close) -  8 seconds, same setup
3. Start screen recording on the phone:
  - Show Discord channel (Segment 2)
  - Open 531 Strength, go through a session (Segment 3)
4. Stop screen recording

### Editing (CapCut is fine -  free, phone-native)

1. Drop face-cam Segment 1
2. Drop Discord screen recording (Segment 2) -  cut to 8 seconds
3. Drop app screen recording (Segment 3) -  cut to 18 seconds
4. Drop face-cam Segment 4
5. Add text overlays on every claim (the text is non-optional -  73% of Shorts are watched muted)
6. Add text overlay for CTA (Segment 5)
7. Check total duration: 42–45 seconds
8. Export portrait (9:16)

### After posting

- Pin the first comment immediately
- Add the channel link to bio on every platform you have
- Link from 531strength.com/process if there's a "watch" section

## What this video does for subsequent Shorts

Once this exists, the other four video ideas from the content plan become easy to produce because they reference this one:

**Video 2 ("My AI agent texts me"):** Deepens the loop story, can say "if you haven't seen how the loop works, there's a link." References the Discord screen from Segment 2.

**Video 3 ("The expedition field log"):** Deepens the Pocket Cast / lore angle. Can say "I mentioned the field logs -  here's what they actually are."

**Video 4 ("The app that builds itself"):** Crossover for lifters who find the channel through app content. The builder backstory is already established.

**Video 5 ("Why I stopped using Strong"):** Pure competitive angle. References this video's "$120 vs. free" line with a full screen-demo expansion.

The channel identity -  developer who lifts and built something unusual -  is set from video one. That's the whole job of this Short.

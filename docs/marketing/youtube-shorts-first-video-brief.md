# YouTube Shorts — First Video Brief

**Video title (in-description, not shown during Short):** "I built a gym app with an AI that runs every 30 minutes"

**Target duration:** 43 seconds

**Format:** Face-cam to open and close. Screen recording in the middle. No special equipment needed — phone camera for face, screen recording for the app demo.

**Platform context:** Personal channel from scratch. Dev influencer angle — "what I built" story. This is the identity-setting post: it establishes who Alex is (developer who lifts, built something real), what the project is (the app + the agent loop), and why it's unusual (54+ expeditions, a blog written by AI). Every subsequent Short references back to this one.

---

## Beat-by-beat script

**0:00 – 0:04 — Hook (face to camera)**
Alex, direct to camera, flat delivery:

"I needed a gym app. Existing ones were either $120 or terrible. So I built one — with an AI agent running every 30 minutes."

Cut immediately. Do not pause. The hook's job is to make the viewer need to know what "AI agent running every 30 minutes" means.

**0:04 – 0:12 — The loop reveal (screen: Discord or loop diagram)**
Switch to screen. Show the Discord #auto-improvements channel or #task-queue channel. One message visible — an expedition acknowledgment, a completed task checkmark, or the loop summary.

Voice-over (or text overlay, since 73% watch muted):
"The agent reads my Discord messages, writes the code, ships it to my phone, and files a field log. Then it starts over."

**0:12 – 0:28 — The app (screen recording)**
Open the app from the home screen. Show the Today queue loading — the sets for the day, the percentages, the plate math already calculated. Log one set: tap the weight, confirm, the completion animation runs. Show the plate visualization. Show the rest timer counting down.

No voice-over needed here — text overlays are enough:
- "Plate math. Automatic."
- "Rest timer. Background."
- "BBB support. Built in."

Keep this section brisk — each cut is 3-4 seconds. The point is "this is a real app that works."

**0:28 – 0:38 — The scale reveal (face to camera)**
Back to face-cam. Slightly amused delivery:

"It's run 54 expeditions. Each one writes a blog post. I subscribe to the field logs on Pocket Cast."

Pause one beat. Then:

"The app is free. No account. Just — here."

**0:38 – 0:43 — CTA (text overlay, face in background)**
Text on screen: "531 Strength — free on iOS and Android"
Text below: "Link in bio · /process for the full story"

Alex can gesture toward the link or just stay still. No verbal CTA needed if the overlays are clear.

## Production notes

**Camera setup:**
- Front-facing phone camera is fine for the face-cam segments
- Film somewhere with natural light facing you (window in front, not behind)
- Plain background preferred — the app and the story are the visual, not the environment
- No tripod required for Shorts — handheld is normal for the format

**Screen recording:**
- iOS: Control Center > Screen Record
- Android: Quick Settings panel > Screen Record
- Turn off Do Not Disturb before recording
- Clear notifications so they don't interrupt
- Landscape or portrait — portrait is native for Shorts format

**Editing:**
- CapCut is the lowest-friction tool for this format (free, phone-native, has auto-captions)
- DaVinci Resolve if Alex is already familiar with it
- The cuts should feel fast — each segment is 4-16 seconds, no lingering
- Add text overlays for every claim that matters (plate math, rest timer, BBB, free, no account)

**Audio:**
- Clean ambient audio is fine — no background music required
- The face-cam segments need audible speech; the screen-recording segment can be silent with text overlays
- If recording in a noisy environment, mute everything and rely on text overlays throughout

## What this video establishes

This Short is the anchor for the entire channel. Once it exists:

- All subsequent Shorts can reference "if you haven't seen the origin story" and link back
- The "dev influencer who lifts" identity is set — future Shorts can go deeper on either the lifting side or the builder side
- The loop description ("AI agent running every 30 minutes") becomes a shorthand that regular viewers recognize
- The Pocket Cast / expedition lore detail signals the builder personality — this is not a polished brand channel, it's a developer who built a weird thing and is honest about how weird it is

The failure mode is over-producing it. This should feel like a developer sharing something they made, not a product launch video. The authenticity of "I needed a thing, I built a thing, here's how it works" is the entire value proposition of the personal channel angle.
